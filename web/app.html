How Inkwell reads a policy

A RoBERTa model fine-tuned on 10,000+ privacy-policy paragraphs (OPP-115, PolicyIE and GoPPC) sorts every paragraph into five topics: what's collected, sharing, your rights, security and retention, and policy changes.

The report card then checks the exact wording of each paragraph. Nothing is generated or summarized.

Full breakdown

Not legal advice. Every quote is an exact sentence from the text you pasted; nothing is generated. “Not mentioned” means we found no matching wording, and the policy may still cover it in other words.

Your text is analyzed and discarded: it is never logged or stored. If you save a policy, we keep its name, the 8 answers, your choice and ticked steps, and a fingerprint of the text to spot changes, never the text itself.
