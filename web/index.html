How it works

From fine print to plain answers in three steps

  1. 1

    Paste the policy

    Open the privacy policy link in any site's footer, press Ctrl+A, copy and paste. Or upload a .txt file.

  2. 2

    We read every sentence

    Our AI model, trained on 10,000+ paragraphs from real privacy policies, sorts every paragraph into five topics: collection, sharing, your rights, security and retention, and policy changes. Then we check the wording: sale, ads, location, deletion and more.

  3. 3

    Decide, your way

    See what the policy allows, which parts you can switch off, and the exact sentence behind each answer. Then take the steps, agree anyway, or walk away. Your call.

About

Built by Token Economy

Inkwell is made by Token Economy, a student team at the University of Maryland. We think you shouldn't need a law degree to know what happens to your data, so we're building the tool we wanted: honest answers, with proof, before you agree.

Our privacy promise

We practise what we check

  • We don't store what you paste. Policies are analyzed in memory and discarded; they're never logged.
  • We don't sell or share your data, and we don't show ads.
  • Accounts hold your sign-in details (name and email, through Google Firebase), your priorities, and the policies you choose to save: a name, the 8 answers, your choices and a fingerprint of the text to spot changes. Never the text itself.
  • To stop abuse of free checks, we count checks per IP address, in memory, for up to an hour.

FAQ

Questions people ask

Is this legal advice?

No. Inkwell helps you find and understand what a policy says. For legal decisions, talk to a lawyer.

Do you use ChatGPT or another AI to write the answers?

No. We use our own classification model, a RoBERTa model fine-tuned on privacy policies, that sorts paragraphs into five topics, plus wording checks. Nothing is generated: every quote is copied straight from the policy.

How accurate is it?

On paragraphs it never saw in training, the topic model scores a macro-F1 of 0.80. The checks look for specific wording, so a policy that says something in unusual words can slip past us. That's why every verdict shows its sentence, and why “Not mentioned” means we found no matching wording, not that the company doesn't do it.

Will Inkwell tell me whether to accept a policy?

No. We show what a policy allows, which parts you can switch off and how, with the exact sentence as proof. You tell us what matters to you, and you make the call: take the steps, agree anyway, or walk away.

Why do I need an account for the full report?

It keeps Inkwell free while we grow, and lets us bring you the extension and change alerts first. It costs nothing.

Which policies work?

Any privacy policy in English that you can copy as text: up to 200 paragraphs, and 300 KB on a free check or 1 MB with an account.
