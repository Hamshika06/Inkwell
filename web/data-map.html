Where does your data go?

Policies bury it in sentences like “we may share with trusted partners”. Inkwell draws it as a map: you, the company, and everyone the policy says gets your data. Then it shows which lines you can cut.

Map a policy nowTry it on the sample

Example: Pigeonpost, a made-up app. Tap anyone on the map.

Reading the map

Red line

Your data goes there, and the policy gives you a way to stop it. Fix it takes you straight to the setting or writes the request.

Grey line

Your data goes there as part of running the service, like hosting or payments. Your choice is whether to use it.

Dashed line

Only in some cases, such as when the law requires it or if the company is sold.

Dotted line

You already limited it. Your exposure score went down when you did.

Every name on the map comes from a sentence in the policy, and tapping it shows that sentence. If a policy doesn't name who it shares with, the map says so instead of guessing.
