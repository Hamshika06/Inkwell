The Inkwell extension for Chrome

Check the policy right on the sign-up page, before you click “I agree”. No copying links, no switching tabs.

  • One click checks the site you're on
  • A small card of what's worth knowing
  • One switch says “don't sell my data” to every site

Add it to your browser · about 2 minutes

  1. Download the extension A small zip file (about 30 KB). Unzip it: you'll get a folder called inkwell-extension. Download the zip
  2. Open your extensions page Paste this into your address bar and press Enter. Browsers don't let websites open it for you. chrome://extensions
  3. Turn on Developer mode The switch is in the top-right corner of that page.
  4. Click “Load unpacked” Choose the inkwell-extension folder you unzipped.
  5. Pin it Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar, then the pin next to Inkwell. You're done: visit any site and click the Inkwell icon.

Works in Chrome, and in Edge and Brave too (use edge://extensions or brave://extensions).
