New

The Inkwell extension for Chrome

Check a site's privacy policy before you sign up, without leaving the page.

  • Shows nothing until you click it. Then one button checks the policy of the site you're on.
  • A small card: what's worth knowing, what isn't clearly answered, and what's in your favour.
  • Fix it: one switch tells every site not to sell or share your data (Global Privacy Control), plus the right settings links and ready-to-send requests with reminders.

It isn't in the Chrome Web Store yet. Until it is, ask the Inkwell team for the install file.
